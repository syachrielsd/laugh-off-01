# Screening — One-Line Laugh-Off

Winner picked by scoring each candidate against the judge's 5 criteria IN ORDER
(funny first, then laugh, surprise, economy, freshness). The first criterion
where candidates differ decides. One idea per line, concrete images, punch on
the last words, no setup notes, no explanation.

## Candidates

1. My therapist is a goldfish. He forgets everything I say. That's why I pay him.
2. My dog fetched the mailman's slippers. Then the mailman's mail. Then the mailman.
3. I started charging my ghost rent. Now he complains about the neighbors.
4. My fridge texted me at 2 a.m.: "We need to talk about the cheese."
5. My GPS sent me through a cornfield, then said "recalculating" like it was my fault.
6. The time traveler aced the interview. He'd done it yesterday.
7. A mime was arrested for assault. He swears he never touched anyone.
8. My houseplant filed for emancipation.
9. The clown got thrown out of the funeral. He was only trying to lighten the mood.
10. I smashed my alarm clock. It was the only thing in my life that ever showed up on time.
11. My Roomba filed a noise complaint against the vacuum.
12. I wrote twelve jokes for this contest. This one won. The other eleven are furious.

## Scoring

Scored 1-5 per criterion, in the judge's order. First differing criterion decides.

## Winner rationale

Candidate 2 wins on the first criterion where the field separates: it is the
funniest line on a cold read, and the hardest, most sudden laugh. The rule of
three does the work — slippers (the classic dog trick), mail (one step up),
mailman (the snap) — and the final word is the punch: a dog dragging a whole
mailman to your door is an instant, concrete image that needs no explanation
and no second thought. Surprise holds because the reader expects a third
object, not a person. Economy is clean at 81 characters with nothing left to
trim. Freshness is the one place it does not lead, but freshness is last in
the judge's order and the dog-fetch escalation, while a known shape, is a
fresh specific line, not a recycled setup.

## Winner

My dog fetched the mailman's slippers. Then the mailman's mail. Then the mailman.
